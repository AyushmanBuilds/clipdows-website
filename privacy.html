Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: October 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Ayushmanbuilds ("we", "us"), based in Berhampur, Odisha, India, collects, uses and protects information when you use ClipDows: the Windows clipboard manager, the ClipDows phone web app (PWA), and the website at https://clipdows.vercel.app (together, the "Service"). Contact: {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}.

In short: your clipboard history is stored on your own PC. Clips you choose to sync are encrypted before upload with a passphrase only you hold. Passwords, OTPs, cards and API keys are masked and never synced. We use your Google account only to sign you in. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

2. Google user data and Limited Use

ClipDows uses Google Sign-In to authenticate you. We request only the basic scopes openid, email and profile. We do not access your Gmail, Drive, Contacts, Calendar or any other Google service data.

  • We use Google user data only to provide and improve the sign-in, account and subscription features you see in ClipDows.
  • ClipDows's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • We do not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or transfer it to data brokers.
  • No human reads Google user data except with your consent, for security or abuse investigation, to comply with law, or where required for support you requested.

You can revoke ClipDows's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and request deletion of your account data as described in section 7.

3. How we use information

  • Provide, secure and maintain the Service, including sign-in, sync and device pairing.
  • Process subscriptions, trials and referral rewards.
  • Diagnose bugs, prevent abuse and measure overall reliability.
  • Send service messages, and notifications you have enabled.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

4. Who we share data with

We do not sell personal information. We share it only with service providers that run the Service for us, under their own privacy terms: Google (Firebase authentication, database, functions and notifications), Razorpay (payments), Vercel (website hosting) and GitHub (software downloads). We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect rights and safety.

5. Encryption and security

Synced clips are encrypted before upload and the passphrase is not available to us, so we cannot recover it or decrypt your clips if you lose it. Data in transit uses HTTPS. No system is perfectly secure; please keep your passphrase and devices protected.

6. Sensitive data controls

ClipDows detects likely passwords, OTPs, card numbers and API keys, masks them, expires them on the schedule you set, and does not sync them. You can add apps (such as password managers) to Ignored apps so their copies are never recorded.

7. Retention and deletion

  • Clipboard history stays on your PC until you delete it or uninstall the app. Deleted items go to Trash first.
  • You can unlink any phone from Settings → Sync & Devices.
  • To delete your account and the data we hold (account, device, subscription and synced data), email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} from your registered address. We act on verified requests within 30 days, except records we must keep for legal or accounting reasons.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the GDPR/UK GDPR and the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, withdraw consent, and complain to a regulator. Email us to exercise these rights. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context advertising.

9. Cookies and website storage

The website stores only your theme choice in your browser's local storage and uses no advertising cookies. Fonts load from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads.

10. Children

ClipDows is not directed to children under 13, and anyone under 18 should use it with a parent or guardian's consent. We do not knowingly collect data from children; contact us and we will delete it.

11. International transfers

Our providers may process data in countries other than yours, including India and the United States. We rely on provider safeguards for such transfers.

12. Changes

We will post updates here and change the date above. Material changes will also be announced in the app or on the website.
