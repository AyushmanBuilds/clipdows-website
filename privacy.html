Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: October 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Ayushmanbuilds ("we", "us"), based in Berhampur, Odisha, India, collects, uses and protects information when you use ClipDows: the Windows clipboard manager, the ClipDows phone web app (PWA), and the website at https://clipdows.vercel.app (together, the "Service"). The Windows app is available from the Microsoft Store, our website and GitHub. Contact: ayushmanbuilds@gmail.com.

In short: your clipboard history is stored on your own PC. Clips you choose to sync are encrypted before upload with a passphrase only you hold. Passwords, OTPs, cards and API keys are masked and never synced. We use your email address only for your account, sign-in and service messages. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

2. Sign-in, email verification and passwords

ClipDows uses Firebase Authentication (a Google service) for email-and-password accounts. We do not offer sign-in with Google or any other social account, and we do not request or access Gmail, Drive, Contacts, Calendar, Photos or any other Google service content.

When you create an account we collect your email address and password. The password is handled and hashed by Firebase Authentication; we cannot read it. We send you an email to verify your address and, if you ask, a password-reset email. Your account record (email, verification status, plan, trial and expiry dates, paired devices) is stored with Google Firebase and our backend over HTTPS so your account works across devices. We do not use it to serve ads, build advertising profiles, or sell or rent it.

Retention and deletion: we keep account details while your account is active. To delete your account, email ayushmanbuilds@gmail.com from your registered address. After we verify the request, we delete the account data we control within 30 days, except records we must retain for legal, security or accounting reasons.

3. How we use information

  • Provide, secure and maintain the Service, including sign-in, sync and device pairing.
  • Verify your email address, which is required to start the free trial and to buy a paid plan.
  • Process subscriptions, trials and referral rewards.
  • Diagnose bugs, prevent abuse and measure overall reliability.
  • Send service messages, and notifications you have enabled.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

4. Who we share data with

We do not sell personal information. We share it only with service providers that run the Service for us, under their own privacy terms: Google (Firebase authentication, database, functions and notifications), Razorpay (payments), Vercel (website hosting) and GitHub (software downloads) and Microsoft (Microsoft Store distribution, under Microsoft's own privacy statement). We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect rights and safety.

5. Encryption and security

Synced clips are encrypted before upload and the passphrase is not available to us, so we cannot recover it or decrypt your clips if you lose it. Data in transit uses HTTPS. No system is perfectly secure; please keep your passphrase and devices protected.

6. Sensitive data controls

ClipDows detects likely passwords, OTPs, card numbers and API keys, masks them, expires them on the schedule you set, and does not sync them. You can add apps (such as password managers) to Ignored apps so their copies are never recorded.

7. Retention and deletion

  • Clipboard history stays on your PC until you delete it or uninstall the app. Deleted items go to Trash first.
  • You can unlink any phone from Settings → Sync & Devices.
  • To delete your account and the data we hold (account, device, subscription and synced data), email ayushmanbuilds@gmail.com from your registered address. We act on verified requests within 30 days, except records we must keep for legal or accounting reasons.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the GDPR/UK GDPR and the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, withdraw consent, and complain to a regulator. Email us to exercise these rights. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context advertising.

9. Cookies and website storage

The website stores only your theme choice in your browser's local storage and uses no advertising cookies. Fonts load from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads.

10. Children

ClipDows is not directed to children under 13, and anyone under 18 should use it with a parent or guardian's consent. We do not knowingly collect data from children; contact us and we will delete it.

11. International transfers

Our providers may process data in countries other than yours, including India and the United States. We rely on provider safeguards for such transfers.

12. Changes

We will post updates here and change the date above. Material changes will also be announced in the app or on the website.
